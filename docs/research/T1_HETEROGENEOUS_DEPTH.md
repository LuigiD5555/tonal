# T1 — Heterogeneous depth

**Status:** frozen experiment line.

## Question

Does heterogeneous composition of Tlaloques degrade less as workflow depth increases than monolithic Parrot or a decomposed but Parrot-centric workflow?

## R2 rule

Architecture R2 may wrap or reproduce T1 through compatibility interfaces, but must not alter T1 workflows, corpus, accounting, preregistered arms or evaluation semantics.

New mechanisms belong to later experiments.

## Role in the broader program

T1 establishes whether externalized heterogeneous composition changes the degradation curve with depth. It does not test adaptive routing, learned primitive discovery, Shponglese, Cognitive JIT or Origami.
