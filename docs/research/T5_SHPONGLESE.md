# T5 — Shponglese semantic IR

**Status:** planned.

## Question

Can a learned or curated primitive/motif vocabulary generalize compositionally across unseen combinations while preserving semantics across codecs?

## Initial semantic hierarchy

```text
ATOM -> MOTIF -> GRAPH -> PACK
```

Shponglese begins as a semantic operational IR, not as a claim of emergent language.

## Required evidence before stronger language claims

- compositionality;
- systematicity;
- slot generalization;
- held-out compositions;
- codec invariance;
- executable semantic equivalence.

The same Shponglese semantics should be testable through conventional text/JSON/binary codecs and through Origami without binding meaning to a carrier.
